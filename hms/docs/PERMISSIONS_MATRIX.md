# Role & permission matrix

> Generated by `npm run docs:permissions` from `src/server/authz/*.ts`. Do not edit by hand.
> These are **protective default templates** for review by hospital management before go-live.
> Changes in a running system are made through *Roles & permissions*, which requires a second authorised approver.

## Scope rules (enforced server-side)

- Every grant is scoped to one branch or explicitly to all branches; grants with neither are ignored (fail closed).
- Grants are effective-dated; expired delegations and revoked roles stop applying on the next request.
- Patient records are visible only in branches where the patient registered or has an appointment/encounter; out-of-scope records appear as *not found*.
- Clinical record access additionally requires a care relationship (care team), `clinical.read.branch_wide`, or an active, reviewed break-glass grant.
- When MFA is enforced, roles marked MFA are suspended until the user enrols.
- Users cannot change their own roles, status or MFA; merges, role changes, guardian verification and break-glass reviews need a second person.

## Permission descriptions

- `settings.read` (ADMIN): View hospital configuration
- `settings.manage` (ADMIN): Change hospital, branch, department, ward, bed and visit-type configuration
- `setup.verify` (ADMIN): Verify or reject imported public facts in the setup checklist
- `users.read` (ADMIN): View staff accounts and their role assignments
- `users.manage` (ADMIN): Create staff accounts, assign/revoke roles, suspend accounts
- `users.credentials.reset` (ADMIN): Issue password resets and clear MFA for staff accounts
- `roles.read` (ADMIN): View role definitions
- `roles.request_change` (ADMIN): Propose changes to role permissions
- `roles.approve_change` (ADMIN): Approve or reject proposed role permission changes
- `sessions.manage` (ADMIN): View and revoke other users' sessions
- `audit.read` (ADMIN): View the audit trail (identifiers only, no clinical content)
- `audit.breakglass.review` (ADMIN): Review emergency (break-glass) access events
- `patient.search` (PHI): Search the patient registry (masked summaries)
- `patient.read` (PHI): View patient demographics and contact details
- `patient.create` (PHI): Register new patients
- `patient.update` (PHI): Edit patient demographics and contacts
- `patient.identifiers.read` (PHI): View unmasked identity document numbers
- `patient.status.manage` (PHI): Mark patients inactive or deceased
- `patient.merge.request` (PHI): Raise or request merge of suspected duplicate records
- `patient.merge.approve` (PHI): Approve a requested merge (must differ from requester)
- `patient.allergy.record` (CLINICAL): Record patient-reported allergies
- `patient.allergy.verify` (CLINICAL): Verify or mark allergy entries as entered-in-error
- `patient.consent.manage` (PHI): Record and withdraw patient consents
- `patient.guardian.manage` (PHI): Record guardian/dependent relationships
- `patient.guardian.verify` (PHI): Verify guardian relationships for portal access
- `appointment.read` (PHI): View appointments
- `appointment.manage` (PHI): Book, reschedule, cancel and mark no-show
- `appointment.override` (PHI): Book an overlapping slot with a recorded reason
- `queue.read` (PHI): View visit queues
