FAQs

Do I need a card to start?

No. Your first month is free and we don't ask for a card. When the free month ends, you pay by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack.

What happens when the free month ends?

We remind you a few days before. Your staff can always clock in and sell, even if a payment is late. You'll see a reminder until it's paid.

What phones does it work on?

Any Android phone or iPhone with a browser, and any computer. Nothing to install from the app store. It's light, so it works well on cheaper phones.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Clock-ins and sales are saved on the phone and sent when the signal comes back. Receipts go out as soon as you're online.

Can staff cheat the clock-in?

Pinga checks the GPS location and takes a selfie at every clock-in. Anything unusual, like clocking in away from the shop, is flagged for you to check.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, any time in Setup → Billing. Moving up to Business never costs more for the same set-up.

How do my staff join?

You type their name and phone number. Pinga makes a link you send on WhatsApp. They open it, choose a PIN, and sign in with their phone number.

Is my data safe?

Each business only ever sees its own data. Staff see only what they need; managers see only their shops. Your data is stored securely.
